Privacy Policy for Insect Hunter

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Insect Hunter respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Insect Hunter mobile game.

Information We Collect

Insect Hunter does not directly collect, store, or share personal information from users.

Game Data

Game-related information such as scores, settings, and progress may be stored locally on your device when necessary for the operation of the game. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

Internet and Third-Party Services

Insect Hunter does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Children's Privacy

Insect Hunter does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.
